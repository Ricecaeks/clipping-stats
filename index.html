clipping stats

clipping stats is a small private tool. Its operator uses it to read YouTube Analytics (views and the country breakdown of viewers) for YouTube channels they own, so they can see where their audience is.

It is not offered to the public and has no users other than its operator's own channels.

This page also forwards Meta (Instagram) sign-ins to its operator's own PC, so the operator can read the insights of their own Instagram accounts.
