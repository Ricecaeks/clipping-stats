Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026.

What it accesses. With your permission, clipping stats reads YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time and viewer country) and your channel's basic details for YouTube channels you sign in with. It requests read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly, youtube.readonly) and never uploads, edits or deletes anything.

How it is used. The data is used only to show the channel owner where their viewers are. It is stored privately on the operator's own computer and is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Who uses it. Only the operator, for channels they own. No one else's data is collected.

Removing access. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; no further data is read after that, and the operator deletes the stored data for that channel on request.

clipping stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. Use the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
